Most back and forth comes from a missing site name. One extra line usually saves a day.

MailView for Jira and Jira Service Management

Send us what you saw, not the email itself. MailView runs inside your Jira site and we have no access to your data, so a description is all we need.

  • Your Atlassian site URL (yoursite.atlassian.net) and the work item key, for example SUP-123
  • The file type (.msg, .eml, or no extension) and its size
  • What the panel showed: the exact message text, or "no panel"
  • Whether it happened in Jira, in the JSM agent view, or in the full view (Open in full view)
  • Your browser and whether an ad blocker or strict privacy setting is on
  • If you can share the file: only a test email or one without personal data, never a real customer email

MailView for Confluence

Same as above: a description, not the email.

  • Your Atlassian site URL and the page URL
  • Whether you used the "Email (MailView)" macro or "Emails on this page"
  • The file type (.msg, .eml, or no extension) and its size
  • What you saw on the page and in the PDF or Word export, if that is where it went wrong

AskSpace for Confluence

  • Your Atlassian site URL and the space key
  • Whether you are a space admin or a regular user
  • The ask and answer permissions for that space
  • A screenshot of the question or the list

Mermaid Live Preview

  • Your Atlassian site URL and the page title

Questions that come up most

AskSpace is not visible in my space

Question and answer is per space and off by default. A space admin turns it on, then sets who may ask and who may answer.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Paid Dortus apps are billed through the Atlassian Marketplace, for Jira and for Confluence alike. Cancel there. Refunds follow Atlassian's policy. Email us if an invoice looks wrong.

MailView does not appear on a work item

It only appears on work items with an .msg or .eml attachment, or a file without a known file type (such as a forwarded email without an extension). On such a work item, look on the right, below the fields, for the MailView box; it may be collapsed, and in Jira's new work item view it sits inside the Apps group there. For the wide panel, add MailView under Apps (in the new view: the app-actions icon next to the title), or ask a Jira admin to turn the panel on for every work item with an email attachment from the panel menu: Show for all work items. Files over 10 MB show a download link instead. It is not available in the JSM customer portal.
