Tell us where you are and what you saw

Most back and forth comes from a missing store or site name. One extra line usually saves a day.

Arrears for Shopify

  • Your store domain, for example acme.myshopify.com
  • The screen: aging, company, statements, reminders, payments, holds
  • The company name or order number involved
  • What you expected versus what the screen showed

AskSpace for Confluence

  • Your Atlassian site URL and the space key
  • Whether you are a space admin or a regular user
  • The ask and answer permissions for that space
  • A screenshot of the question or the list

Mermaid Live Preview

  • Your Atlassian site URL and the page title
  • The diagram source that fails to render
  • The diagram type: flowchart, sequence, or timeline
  • Any error text shown inside the macro

Questions that come up most

Arrears shows a balance I do not recognise

Arrears builds aging from Shopify payment schedules. If an order was paid outside Shopify, record that payment in Arrears so the allocation matches your bank.

A reminder did not go out

Check the reminder history on the company. Manual sends need approval in the Ready queue, and daily automatic sending is a paid plan feature.

A held company still reaches checkout

Holds turn B2B checkout into a draft order. Send us the store domain and the company name and we will trace the specific attempt.

AskSpace is not visible in my space

Question and answer is per space and off by default. A space admin turns it on, then sets who may ask and who may answer.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Billing runs through the platform: Shopify for Arrears, the Atlassian Marketplace for the Confluence apps. Cancel there, and email us if an invoice looks wrong.
