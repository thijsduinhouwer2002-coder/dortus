Support

Every Dortus tool is made by Thijs, and he reads every mail himself. A question, an idea or a bug? Write to thijs@dortus.com, in English or Dutch. Add the few details for your tool below and you save a round of mails.

We aim to reply within 5 working days.

Click Joints and Click Joints Pro

Blender add-on for poseable 3D prints.

  • Your Blender version, and whether you use the free version or Pro
  • What you did and what happened, with a screenshot if you can
  • If the joint comes out wrong: the clearance in mm and, if you can share it, a small .blend file

Bought Pro? Your download link is in the mail from Gumroad. Bugs and ideas can also go to GitHub issues. Privacy.

Ebb for Garmin

Breathing app for Garmin watches.

  • Your watch model
  • What you did and what happened

Refunds follow the Garmin Connect IQ Store: a 48-hour return window after you buy. Safety notes and the watch list are on the Ebb support page. Privacy.

Rollwright

  • Your Obsidian version and Rollwright version (Settings, Community plugins)

  • What you expected and what you got

Bugs and ideas can also go to GitHub issues. Privacy.
