How every Dortus app is built

Runs on Atlassian. Built on Atlassian Forge. The apps run inside Atlassian's cloud, and Dortus has no servers for them.

No content to Dortus. The apps read your data with your own permissions, and none of it is sent to Dortus. AskSpace and Mermaid Live Preview keep their data in Atlassian's Forge storage for your site. MailView for Jira counts how often it is used, as three numbers per site. What each app stores.

Plain English. Every warning, verdict and setting is written for the person who has to act on it. Support is answered by the developer.

MailView

Email attachments you can actually read, in Jira and Confluence

Jira and Confluence show an Outlook .msg as code and a .eml as raw text. MailView shows the email as it was sent, with inline images and the files inside, and adds a plain-English check for suspicious mail: SPF, DKIM and DMARC results from the headers, domains with lookalike letters from other alphabets or with accents, and the address behind each link.
