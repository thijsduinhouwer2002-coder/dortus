MailView

Email attachments you can actually read, in Jira and Confluence

Jira and Confluence show an Outlook .msg as code and a .eml as raw text. MailView shows the email as it was sent, with inline images and the files inside, and adds a plain-English check for suspicious mail: SPF, DKIM and DMARC results, lookalike domains, and where each link really goes.

MailView for Jira and Jira Service Management

Reads .msg and .eml attachments on the work item, also in the JSM agent view. Includes forwarded emails attached without a file extension.
