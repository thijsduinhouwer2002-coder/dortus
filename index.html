Calm, focused apps for Confluence

Dortus builds small Atlassian apps that do one job well. Our first app is AskSpace: Q&A for each Confluence space.

Meet AskSpace

Coming soon to the Atlassian Marketplace.

Q&A where each team already works

Give every Confluence space its own questions and answers. Space admins decide who may ask, who may answer and who moderates.

  • One click per space

    Turn Q&A on only where it helps.

  • You decide who answers

    Separate permissions for asking and answering, plus moderators.

  • Answers people can find

    Search runs through questions and answers, and similar questions show up while you type.
