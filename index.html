Dortus makes editors for Confluence.

Diagrams and formulas, written as text. Type Mermaid or LaTeX, or paste it from ChatGPT or Claude, and the picture draws as you type. Everything runs on Atlassian: no Dortus servers, and none of your content leaves your site.

The editors

Both work the same way: code on the left, the result on the right, a clear message when something is wrong, and earlier versions one click away. The result is saved in the page, so it shows up in search, exports and copies of the page like any other content.

Mermaid Editor for Confluence

Flowcharts, sequence diagrams, Gantt charts, mind maps and more, from a few lines of text.

  • Twelve examples to start from, or paste what an AI wrote
  • Errors say what is wrong and mark the line
  • Zoom, open large, download as SVG or PNG
  • The last 20 versions of every diagram

Math for Confluence

Equations on their own line or inside a sentence, written in LaTeX and drawn as you type.

  • Fractions, matrices, aligned equations and chemistry
  • A typo shows its line and column, and the last good version stays visible
  • Paste with $$ around it; the delimiters are removed for you
  • Readable in light and dark mode, with MathML for screen readers
