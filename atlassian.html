All Dortus apps

Dortus builds editors for Confluence Cloud: write it as text, see it on the page. A few older apps are listed further down for the sites that use them. Every app runs on Atlassian Forge, and Dortus has no servers for them.

The editors

Type Mermaid, PlantUML, Markdown or LaTeX, or paste it from ChatGPT or Claude, and the result draws as you type.

Mermaid Editor for Confluence

Flowcharts, sequence diagrams, Gantt charts, mind maps and more, from a few lines of text. The source stays editable in the page.

Free on the Atlassian Marketplace

Get Mermaid Editor → How it works

PlantUML Editor for Confluence

Sequence, class, activity, C4 and more, drawn as you type. No PlantUML server: the diagram is drawn in the browser.

Free on the Atlassian Marketplace

Get PlantUML Editor → How it works

Math for Confluence

LaTeX equations on their own line or inside a sentence, drawn as you type. A typo shows its line and column.

Other apps

Earlier Dortus apps. They keep working for the sites that use them, and questions about them go to the same support address.

  • MailView for Jira

    Reads .msg and .eml email attachments on a Jira work item, with a plain-English phishing check.

  • MailView for Confluence

    Shows .msg and .eml email attachments on a Confluence page, also in PDF and Word exports.

  • AskSpace

    Questions and answers for each Confluence space, with votes and one best answer.

  • Live Countdown for Confluence

    Counts down to a release, launch or deadline on a Confluence page. Waiting for Atlassian's approval.

  • Mermaid Live Preview for Jira

    Mermaid diagrams on a Jira work item. Waiting for Atlassian's approval.

  • Email check

    Check a .msg or .eml file or pasted headers for phishing, in your browser, on this site.

Your content stays with Atlassian

Every Dortus app runs on Atlassian Forge. Dortus operates no server for them and receives none of your content. Where an app keeps data, such as version history, it stays in Forge app storage in your Atlassian site. The privacy policy lists what each app stores.
