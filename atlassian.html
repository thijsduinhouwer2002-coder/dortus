The apps

Four apps are on the Atlassian Marketplace. Live Countdown and Mermaid Live Preview for Jira are waiting for Atlassian's approval. Each app is a separate install.

MailView for Jira

Reads .msg and .eml email attachments on the Jira work item, also in the Jira Service Management agent view, with a plain-English phishing check.

Available on the Atlassian Marketplace

About MailView for Jira →

MailView for Confluence

Shows .msg and .eml email attachments on the Confluence page, keeps them readable in PDF and Word exports, and tells you when an email looks like phishing.

Available on the Atlassian Marketplace

About MailView for Confluence →

Mermaid Editor for Confluence

Type a Mermaid diagram in a Confluence page and watch it draw next to the text, with the source kept editable in the page.

Available on the Atlassian Marketplace

About Mermaid Editor for Confluence →

AskSpace

Gives each Confluence space its own questions and answers, with votes, one best answer and separate permissions for asking and answering.

Available on the Atlassian Marketplace

About AskSpace →

Live Countdown for Confluence

Counts down to a release, a launch, an event or a deadline on a Confluence page, in the right time zone.

Submitted to the Atlassian Marketplace, waiting for approval

About Live Countdown →

Math for Confluence

LaTeX equations on a Confluence page, drawn as you type. On their own line or inside a sentence; nothing leaves Atlassian.

Submitted to the Atlassian Marketplace, waiting for approval

About Math for Confluence →

Mermaid Live Preview for Jira

Mermaid diagrams on a Jira work item, with a live preview as you type. The diagrams are saved on the work item itself.

Submitted to the Atlassian Marketplace, waiting for approval

Your content stays with Atlassian

All Dortus apps run on Atlassian Forge. Dortus operates no server for them and receives no app content. MailView for Jira, MailView for Confluence and Live Countdown store nothing. AskSpace and Mermaid Editor for Confluence keep their data in Forge app storage in your Atlassian site. MailView for Jira also counts how often it is used, as three numbers per site, through Atlassian.
