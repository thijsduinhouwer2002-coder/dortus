Your content never leaves your site

All apps run on Atlassian Forge. MailView stores nothing: it reads the attachment and renders it in your browser (a Confluence PDF or Word export is built by a Forge function inside Atlassian). AskSpace and Mermaid Live Preview keep questions, answers, votes, settings and diagram source in Forge app storage inside your own cloud site. Dortus operates no server for them and receives no app content.
