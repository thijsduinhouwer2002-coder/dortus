Two apps, two jobs

Both are Forge apps. Both keep their data inside your Atlassian cloud site. Neither one asks you to move your documentation anywhere else.

AskSpace

A question and answer board per Confluence space. Ask, answer, vote, and mark one best answer that the asker cannot award to itself. Who may ask and who may answer are two separate settings.

Mermaid Live Preview
