Ebb for Garmin: Support

Help with Ebb, a breathing app for Garmin watches, made by Dortus.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Contact

Mail thijs@dortus.com and tell us your watch model and what happened. We aim to reply within 5 working days.

Refunds

Refunds follow the Garmin Connect IQ Store policy: Garmin gives you a 48-hour return window after you buy.

Safety

Use Ebb on land only, sitting or lying down. Never practise breath holds in or near water, in the bath, or while driving. Feel dizzy or tingly? Stop and breathe normally. Not if pregnant, under 18, or with epilepsy, heart or blood pressure problems. Ebb is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. A hold stops by itself after five minutes.

Which watches

Ebb runs on 28 round Garmin watches. The full list is on the store page.

Privacy

Ebb collects nothing. Read the privacy notice.
