AskSpace vs Questions for Confluence

Questions for Confluence is Atlassian's own Q&A app. AskSpace is a Q&A app per Confluence space, and I build it. If Questions for Confluence works for you, there is no reason to change. If you are looking at alternatives, this page compares the two on five points, says where Questions for Confluence is ahead, and shows how to move.

Side by side

What each app does in Confluence Cloud. For Questions for Confluence this follows Atlassian's documentation; for AskSpace, the version that runs today.

Per spaceOff in every space until a space admin turns it on. Every question belongs to one space.Questions can be asked in a space or globally, for the whole site. Access follows the space's permissions.

Export to CSV.An exporter and importer for Questions in Cloud is an open feature request on Atlassian's tracker (CONFCLOUD-51723).

Both apps have votes on answers and one accepted answer. In Questions for Confluence the person who asked, or an admin, accepts an answer. In AskSpace the person who asked, or a moderator, marks the best answer.

Atlassian's documentation for Questions for Confluence: Ask a question, Assign Questions for Confluence permissions, Integrate questions with your spaces and Use Questions for Confluence.

Where Questions for Confluence is ahead

  • Confluence search. Its questions show up in Confluence's own search, next to pages. AskSpace questions only show up in AskSpace's search.
  • Made by Atlassian. It is Atlassian's own app, documented on Atlassian's support site.
  • Around for years. Atlassian introduced it as Confluence Questions in 2014 (Atlassian blog).
  • Site-wide questions. Questions can also be asked globally, not only inside a space.
  • Popular questions. The Questions List macro can show popular questions. The AskSpace block shows recent, unanswered or solved ones.

If your team uses Questions for Confluence and it works for you, there is no reason to change.

When AskSpace may fit better

  • You want to choose separately who may ask and who may answer.
  • You want search to look inside the answers, not only the questions.
  • Each team should decide for its own space whether to use Q&A.
  • You want a way to get your questions and answers out again, as a CSV file.

AskSpace is listed on the Atlassian Marketplace.

How to move from Questions for Confluence

On the same Confluence Cloud site, AskSpace reads your questions directly. You don't need an export file.

  • Start the import. In a space where you are a space admin, go to Space settings → Integrations → AskSpace, then Import & export. When your site has questions in Questions for Confluence, Questions for Confluence (this site) is one of the sources.
  • What it reads. AskSpace reads the questions, answers, topics and accepted answers, as you. Topics become tags, up to 3 per question.
  • Preview first. You see what was found, grouped by space, before anything is saved. Per space you choose: import into that space, into another space where you are a space admin, or skip.
  • Undo. After the import, Undo this import takes it out again.
  • The originals stay. AskSpace only reads. Nothing changes in Questions for Confluence, so your questions stay there as they were.
  • From Server or Data Center. Use the JSON file from GET /rest/questions/1.0/question?limit=700 on your old Confluence.
