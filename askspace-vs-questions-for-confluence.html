Questions for Confluence vs AskSpace: an honest comparison

Questions for Confluence is Atlassian's own Q&A app for Confluence (Atlassian: Questions for Confluence on the Marketplace). AskSpace is a Q&A app per Confluence space, and I build it at Dortus. If Questions for Confluence works for your team, keep it. This page shows what the two apps share, where Questions for Confluence is ahead, where AskSpace may fit better, and how to move if you decide to.

Written on 14 October 2026. Facts about Questions for Confluence come from Atlassian's pages, linked next to each point; where something comes from our own test, the page says so. Facts about AskSpace describe the version on the Atlassian Marketplace today. Atlassian changes its pages and menu names now and then, so if your site looks different, go by the linked Atlassian page.

What both apps do

Some things look like differences, but both apps have them:

  • A place in the sidebar. Questions for Confluence is under Apps → Questions in the Confluence sidebar (Atlassian: Integrate questions with your spaces). AskSpace opens from the space sidebar.
  • Permissions per space. In Questions for Confluence, access follows the space permissions, and per space you set View, Add and Delete for each group (Atlassian: Assign Questions for Confluence permissions). AskSpace has its own settings per space, split another way. It has no setting for who may view: everyone who can open AskSpace in the space can read its questions and vote. What it splits is asking and answering; see When AskSpace may fit better.
  • Votes and one best answer. People vote answers up or down. In Questions for Confluence they can also vote on questions (Atlassian: What are votes and experts?); in AskSpace only answers get votes. In Questions for Confluence the person who asked accepts an answer, or an admin does (Atlassian: Ask a question). In AskSpace the person who asked marks the best answer, or a moderator does: a space admin, or someone in a moderator group the space admin picks.
  • Questions on a page. Questions for Confluence has the Questions List macro (Atlassian: Integrate questions with your spaces). AskSpace has the AskSpace questions block.

When Questions for Confluence is enough

On these points Questions for Confluence is ahead of AskSpace. If they matter to your team, stay with it.

  • Made by Atlassian. It is Atlassian's own app, with documentation on Atlassian's support site (Atlassian: Questions for Confluence on the Marketplace, Use Questions for Confluence).
  • Questions in Confluence search. Questions show up in Confluence's quick search and full search results (Atlassian: Ask a question, Questions for Confluence on the Marketplace). Atlassian's docs also mention a search filter for questions. AskSpace questions don't show up there: AskSpace has its own search, one space at a time.
  • Questions for the whole site. Besides questions in a space, there are global questions, asked to the whole site (Atlassian: Integrate questions with your spaces). In AskSpace every question belongs to one space.
  • One list across spaces. The Questions List macro can show questions from every space the reader can view, filtered to recent, popular or unanswered questions (Atlassian: Integrate questions with your spaces). The AskSpace block shows up to 10 questions from the space of the page it is on: recent, unanswered or solved.
  • Points and experts. Votes build a reputation, and a leaderboard shows the top experts (Atlassian: What are votes and experts?). AskSpace has no points.
  • Told about new questions. People can watch a topic and get notified when a new question is added to it (Atlassian: Watch and edit topics). AskSpace sends no email, and nobody gets a notice when a new question is asked. Its notices show only inside AskSpace, for example when someone answers your question or marks your answer as best.

If your team asks questions across the whole site, or wants them to turn up next to pages in search, Questions for Confluence is the better fit. There is no reason to switch.

When AskSpace may fit better

Here AskSpace works differently, and that may suit your team better. Each AskSpace point describes the app as it runs today.

  • Separate rules for asking and answering. In Questions for Confluence, anyone who can ask a question can also answer one (Atlassian: Ask a question). In AskSpace a space admin sets Who may ask questions? and Who may answer? separately: everyone in the space, space admins only, or chosen groups. With chosen groups, only members of those groups can ask or answer, space admins included. For example: everyone may ask, and only the support team answers.
  • Off until a space admin turns it on. In Questions for Confluence, anyone with access to a space can view, ask and take part in its questions, and a space admin can take permissions away (Atlassian: Assign Questions for Confluence permissions). In AskSpace, Q&A is off in every space until a space admin turns it on in that space. Until then, the AskSpace page in the space sidebar only says that Q&A is off. There is no site-wide switch, so on a site with many spaces each space admin does this for their own space.
  • Search per space, answers included. AskSpace's search looks in the titles, details, tags and answers of the space's questions, and says Found in an answer when a search word is only found in an answer. Questions for Confluence has its own search too: you view and search a space's questions from Questions in the sidebar (Atlassian: Integrate questions with your spaces), and its listing says you can find questions and answers in Confluence search (Atlassian: Questions for Confluence on the Marketplace). So this is a different way to search, not a missing feature.
  • Import with a preview and an undo. AskSpace imports from Questions for Confluence on the same site, from CSV, and from a Server or Data Center export file. You see a preview before anything is saved, and Undo this import removes the imported questions again. For Questions for Confluence in Cloud, an exporter and importer is an open feature request (Atlassian: CONFCLOUD-51723).
  • Export to CSV. A space admin exports the space's questions and answers to one CSV file, and AskSpace can import that file again. Questions for Confluence has no export in Cloud: Atlassian says there is currently no way to export Questions data from Confluence Cloud (Atlassian: Migrate from Confluence Cloud to Data Center, Confluence Questions FAQ). To keep a copy anyway: on a Cloud site we tested, the questions and answers are Confluence content that Confluence's general REST API can read (Atlassian: Confluence REST API, content). Atlassian doesn't offer that as an export. How to export Questions for Confluence Cloud data shows how.

A limit to know. An AskSpace space holds up to 6,000 questions. When a space is full, nobody can ask a new question there.

AskSpace is listed on the Atlassian Marketplace.

How to move from Questions for Confluence

On the same Confluence Cloud site, AskSpace reads your questions directly, with your own account, through Confluence's REST API. You don't need an export file, and you can start with one space. Atlassian doesn't describe this as a way to move Questions data, so check the counts in the preview. AskSpace tells you what it could not bring over, such as questions without a space or questions imported without their best answer.

  • Start the import. You need to be a space admin. Go to Space settings → Integrations → AskSpace. Under Import & export, choose Import questions. When you can see questions in Questions for Confluence on your site, Questions for Confluence (this site) is one of the sources.
  • What it reads. The questions, answers, topics and accepted answers, with their original dates. AskSpace reads them with your own account, so it only brings over what you can see in Confluence. Topics become tags, up to 3 per question, and AskSpace tells you how many topics did not fit.
  • Preview first. Before anything is saved, you see how many questions were found in each space, and the first 5 questions. For each space you choose: import into the same space, into the space you started from or another space in the list, or skip it. You can only import into a space where you are a space admin.
  • What doesn't come along. Votes, and anything that is not a question or an answer: AskSpace has no comments. Images, attachments, mentions of people and macros without their own text become [content omitted during import]; code blocks stay code blocks. A question that Confluence shows without a space can't be imported; the preview counts these.
  • Limits. One import takes up to 5,000 questions and up to 100 answers per question.
  • Undo. Undo this import deletes the imported questions and all their answers, including answers people added after the import. You can't get them back.
  • The originals stay. AskSpace only reads. Nothing changes in Questions for Confluence. The import is a copy, not a sync: run it again later and AskSpace adds only the questions that are new since then. New answers to questions you already imported don't come along.
  • From Server or Data Center. Questions for Confluence on Server and Data Center has a REST API that can export questions; it needs version 2.3.0 or later (Atlassian: How to export Confluence Questions data). AskSpace reads the JSON file from GET /rest/questions/1.0/question?limit=700 on your Server or Data Center site. That file's format isn't documented, so AskSpace reads the fields it knows and leaves the rest empty. If the file lists answers but leaves out their text, AskSpace tells you how to get them. To move to Questions for Confluence in Cloud instead, Atlassian's Confluence Cloud Migration Assistant can migrate your questions (Atlassian: Migrating Questions for Confluence App).

To install AskSpace and turn it on in a space first, see Getting started with AskSpace.

Short answers

Can I use both apps at the same time? Yes. AskSpace only reads from Questions for Confluence, and Q&A stays off in a space until a space admin turns it on. So you can try AskSpace in one space while the rest of the site keeps using Questions for Confluence. The two apps don't sync, so pick one place for new questions in each space.

Do AskSpace questions show up in Confluence search? No. AskSpace has its own search per space. Questions for Confluence questions do show up in Confluence search (Atlassian: Ask a question).

Do votes come along when I import? No. Questions, answers, original dates, topics (as tags) and accepted answers do.

Can I get my questions out of AskSpace again? Yes. A space admin exports the space to CSV: one row per question or answer, with author, date, tags and best answer. Votes are not in the file, and authors are names, not accounts.

Where are AskSpace questions stored? AskSpace runs on Atlassian Forge. Your questions are kept in the app's own storage, hosted by Atlassian, not on Dortus servers. They are not Confluence pages, so use the CSV export when you want your own copy.

Are there other ways to run Q&A in Confluence? Yes. Q&A in Confluence Cloud: your options lists them, How to build an FAQ in Confluence Cloud shows how to do it without an app, and Stack Internal vs Q&A inside Confluence helps if you are weighing a separate tool.
