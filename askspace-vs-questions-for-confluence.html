Questions for Confluence vs AskSpace: an honest comparison

Questions for Confluence is Atlassian's own Q&A app for Confluence. AskSpace is a Q&A app per Confluence space, and I build it at Dortus. If Questions for Confluence works for your team, keep it. This page shows what the two apps share, where Questions for Confluence is ahead, the real differences, and how to move if you decide to.

Written on 14 October 2026. Facts about Questions for Confluence come from Atlassian's documentation and Marketplace listing, linked next to each point. Facts about AskSpace describe the version on the Atlassian Marketplace today. Atlassian changes its pages and menu names now and then, so if your site looks different, go by the linked Atlassian page.

What both apps do

Some things look like differences, but both apps have them:

  • A place in the sidebar. Questions for Confluence is under Apps → Questions in the Confluence sidebar (Atlassian: Integrate questions with your spaces). AskSpace opens from the space sidebar.
  • Permissions per space. In Questions for Confluence, access follows the space permissions, and per space you set View, Add and Delete for each group (Atlassian: Assign Questions for Confluence permissions). AskSpace has its own settings per space. How the permissions are split up is different; see When AskSpace may fit better.
  • Votes and one best answer. People vote answers up or down. In Questions for Confluence the person who asked accepts an answer, or an admin does (Atlassian: Ask a question). In AskSpace the person who asked marks the best answer, or a moderator does.
  • Questions on a page. Questions for Confluence has the Questions List macro (Atlassian: Integrate questions with your spaces). AskSpace has the AskSpace questions block.

When Questions for Confluence is enough

On these points Questions for Confluence is ahead of AskSpace. If they matter to your team, stay with it.

  • Made by Atlassian. It is Atlassian's own app, with its own Marketplace listing and documentation on Atlassian's support site (Atlassian: Use Questions for Confluence).
  • Questions in Confluence search. Questions show up in Confluence's quick search and full search results, with a filter for questions (Atlassian: Ask a question). AskSpace questions don't: AskSpace has its own search, one space at a time.
  • Questions for the whole site. Besides questions in a space, there are global questions, asked to the whole site (Atlassian: Integrate questions with your spaces). In AskSpace every question belongs to one space.
  • One list across spaces. The Questions List macro can show questions from every space the reader can view, and it can show popular questions (Atlassian: Integrate questions with your spaces). The AskSpace block shows the questions of one space: recent, unanswered or solved.
  • Points and experts. Votes build a reputation, and a leaderboard shows the top experts (Atlassian: What are votes and experts?). AskSpace has no points.

If your team asks questions across the whole site, or wants them to turn up next to pages in search, Questions for Confluence is the better fit. There is no reason to switch.

When AskSpace may fit better

These are the real differences. Each AskSpace point describes the app as it runs today.

  • Different people ask and answer. In Questions for Confluence, “any user that can ask a question can answer a question” (Atlassian: Ask a question). In AskSpace a space admin sets Who may ask questions? and Who may answer? separately: everyone in the space, space admins only, or chosen groups. For example: everyone may ask, and only the support team answers.
  • Off until a space turns it on. In Questions for Confluence, “if someone has access to a space, they'll be able to view, ask, and interact with questions in that space”; a space admin can take permissions away (Atlassian: Assign Questions for Confluence permissions). AskSpace is off in every space until a space admin turns it on, so each team decides for its own space.
  • One search box for questions and answers. AskSpace's search looks in the titles, details, tags and answers of the space's questions, and says Found in an answer when only an answer matches. Questions for Confluence uses Confluence search instead, and its listing says you can find questions and answers there (Atlassian: Questions for Confluence on the Marketplace). So this is a different way to search, not a missing feature.
  • Import with a preview and an undo. AskSpace imports from Questions for Confluence on the same site, from a Server or Data Center export file, or from CSV. You see a preview before anything is saved, and Undo this import removes the imported questions again. For Questions for Confluence in Cloud, an exporter and importer is an open feature request (Atlassian: CONFCLOUD-51723).
  • Export to CSV. A space admin exports the space's questions and answers to one CSV file, and AskSpace can import that file again. Atlassian's FAQ says there is currently no way to export content from Questions for Confluence Cloud (Atlassian: Confluence Questions FAQ). The Confluence REST API can still read it: How to export Questions for Confluence Cloud data shows how.

How to move from Questions for Confluence

On the same Confluence Cloud site, AskSpace reads your questions directly. You don't need an export file, and you can start with one space.

  • Start the import. You need to be a space admin. Go to Space settings → Integrations → AskSpace, then Import & export. When your site has questions in Questions for Confluence, Questions for Confluence (this site) is one of the sources.
  • What it reads. The questions, answers, topics and accepted answers, as you: you bring over what your own account can see. The original dates come along. Topics become tags, up to 3 per question, and AskSpace tells you how many topics did not fit.
  • Preview first. Before anything is saved, you see how many questions were found, per space, and the first 5. Per space you choose: import into that space, into another space where you are a space admin, or skip.
  • What doesn't come along. Votes. Images, attachments and mentions in the text become [content omitted during import]. One import takes up to 5,000 questions, with up to 100 answers each, and a space holds up to 6,000 questions.
  • Undo. Undo this import deletes the imported questions with all their answers, also answers people added after the import. That delete is final.
  • The originals stay. AskSpace only reads. Nothing changes in Questions for Confluence, so both can run side by side while you decide.
  • From Server or Data Center. Use the JSON file from GET /rest/questions/1.0/question?limit=700 on your old Confluence. If the file has no answers, AskSpace tells you. To move to Questions for Confluence in Cloud instead, Atlassian's Cloud Migration Assistant can migrate it (Atlassian: Migrating Questions for Confluence App).

Short answers

Can I use both apps at the same time? Yes. AskSpace only reads from Questions for Confluence, and it stays off in a space until a space admin turns it on. So you can try AskSpace in one space while the rest of the site keeps using Questions for Confluence.

Do AskSpace questions show up in Confluence search? No. AskSpace has its own search per space. Questions for Confluence questions do show up in Confluence search (Atlassian: Ask a question).

Do votes come along when I import? No. Questions, answers, original dates, topics (as tags) and accepted answers do.

Can I get my questions out of AskSpace again? Yes. A space admin exports the space to CSV: one row per question or answer, with author, date, tags and best answer. Votes are not in the file.

Where are AskSpace questions stored? AskSpace runs on Atlassian Forge, so your questions are stored by Atlassian with your site, not on Dortus servers.

Are there other ways to run Q&A in Confluence? Yes. Q&A in Confluence Cloud: your options lists them, How to build an FAQ in Confluence Cloud shows how to do it without an app, and Stack Internal vs Q&A inside Confluence helps if you are weighing a separate tool.
