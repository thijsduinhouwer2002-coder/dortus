AskSpace

Q&A for each Confluence space, with separate ask and answer permissions.

Read the docs

Coming soon to the Atlassian Marketplace.

The same questions, again and again

Someone asks in chat, someone answers, and next month the next person asks the same thing. The answer never ends up where people look. AskSpace keeps questions and answers in the Confluence space they belong to, so the next person finds them.

Features

  • Turn it on per space

    A space admin turns on Q&A with one click. Spaces that don’t need it stay untouched.

  • Separate ask and answer permissions

    For each space, choose who may ask and who may answer: everyone in the space, space admins only, or chosen Confluence groups.

  • Moderators

    Space admins are always moderators. Add groups that may edit or remove any question or answer and choose the best answer. Edits show a small “Edited by a moderator” label, and the author is notified.

  • One best answer, plus votes

    The person who asked picks the best answer, but never their own. Moderators can pick or clear it too. Everyone can vote answers up or down.

  • Search that reads the answers

    Search looks through titles, details and answers. If a match is only in an answer, AskSpace tells you.

  • Similar questions while you type

    Before posting, people see up to three similar questions, so duplicates don’t pile up.

  • Tags

    Add up to three tags per question, click a tag to see every question with it, and share the filtered list.

  • A block for any page

    Add the AskSpace block to a space home or onboarding page. It shows recent, unanswered or solved questions, optionally for one tag, with an “Ask a question” button.

  • Links and simple formatting

    Every question has its own link to share. Answers support links, bold, inline code, code blocks and lists.

  • No points, no leaderboards

    AskSpace is about answers, not scores.

  • Never locked in

    Import questions from a CSV file or from Questions for Confluence (Server/Data Center), and export everything to CSV whenever you want. Moving from Questions for Confluence Cloud on the same site takes one click.

Your questions stay in your Atlassian cloud

AskSpace is built on Atlassian Forge and qualifies for Runs on Atlassian. Questions, answers, votes and settings are stored in Forge storage on your site. Nothing is sent to Dortus or anyone else.
