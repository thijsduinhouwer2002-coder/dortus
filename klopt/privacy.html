Klopt for TikTok Shop

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Before launch. Klopt is in development and not yet available. This policy describes how Klopt is being built to handle data. We will check it against the finished app, and update the date above, before Klopt goes live. A Dutch summary is at the end.

1. Who we are

Klopt is made by Thijs Duinhouwer, trading as Dortus, the Netherlands (“Dortus”, “we”). Contact for privacy questions and data requests: thijs@dortus.com. The general Dortus privacy policy covers our other products; this page covers Klopt.

2. What Klopt does

Klopt reads the settlements (payouts) of your TikTok Shop and books them in your Moneybird administration as journal entries: revenue, discounts, refunds, commission, shipping fees and taxes, each on a ledger account you choose, checked against the amount paid out to you.

3. Our role

For your shop and accounting data, you (the seller who installs Klopt) are the controller and Dortus is the processor: we process that data only to show and make the bookings you switch on. For your own contact with us (support email, billing questions), Dortus is the controller of that correspondence.

4. What Klopt reads and stores

From TikTok Shop

  • Your shop ID and shop cipher (the technical identifiers TikTok uses to route requests for your shop), and the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues when you authorise Klopt. Klopt does not store your shop name or seller name.
  • Finance data, read to work out each booking: settlements and their transactions per order, with order IDs, SKU IDs, quantities, amounts, fees, currencies and dates. The finance response from TikTok can also contain product and SKU names; Klopt drops those, and any other field it does not need for the booking, right after reading, so they are never stored.

Klopt asks TikTok only for finance data. It does not ask for buyer names, addresses, phone numbers or email addresses, and it does not store them.

From and to Moneybird

  • The access token Moneybird issues when you connect, and the ID and name of the administration you choose.
  • Your ledger accounts and tax rates, so you can pick where each amount goes.
  • Klopt writes journal entries to your administration with the settlement date, amounts and a reference with the TikTok settlement ID. It writes one line per type of amount; order details stay in Klopt. It stores the ID of each entry it made, so it never books the same settlement twice.

In Klopt itself

  • Your settings: chosen ledger accounts, tax rates and start date.
  • The status of each settlement (for example booked, waiting, set aside) and short technical notes about errors, so you can see what happened.
  • Settlement and order transactions, kept only while a settlement is still being worked out, and deleted as soon as it is booked (or turns out to be empty). If it stops with an error, they are removed by the next clean-up run. A settlement that waits for a subscription keeps them until it is booked or is no longer booked automatically. When you look at how a past settlement was booked, Klopt fetches it again from TikTok.

5. Billing

Subscriptions are sold by Polar (polar.sh), which acts as merchant of record: Polar takes the payment, charges VAT and sends your invoice. Polar processes your name, email address and payment details under its own privacy policy. When you start a subscription, Klopt passes Polar a shop key (an internal Klopt number, not your TikTok shop ID) and your IP address, which Polar uses to determine the VAT country. Klopt stores only your Polar customer ID, your subscription status (whether it is active and until when, its ID, whether it is cancelled, and whether a payment failed), when you first opened checkout, and, for each settlement it booked, the number of orders, to count the free limit. We never see card details, and we do not store amounts paid or your email address. If cancelling your subscription fails when you disconnect, we keep the shop key and your Polar customer ID until the subscription has really stopped, even if that takes longer than 30 days, so that you are not charged again.

6. Where it runs

Klopt runs on Cloudflare Workers, with its data in a Cloudflare D1 database that is encrypted at rest. Our own app keys are kept as Cloudflare secrets, not in source code. The TikTok and Moneybird tokens of your shop are stored in the D1 database, which is encrypted at rest, and Klopt encrypts them once more itself (AES-GCM) before storing them. All traffic uses HTTPS. Access to production is limited to Dortus.

7. Subprocessors

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosting and database.

TikTok Shop and Moneybird are the services you connect yourself; they process your data under their own terms. Polar Software, Inc. sells the subscription as merchant of record and processes billing data under its own privacy policy; Klopt passes Polar only a shop key and, when you start a subscription, your IP address. Some providers are established outside the EU and may process limited data there under their own transfer mechanisms, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

We do not sell data, we do not use advertising networks, and we do not use your shop data for our own marketing or to train AI models.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept while Klopt is connected to your shop.
  • When you disconnect Klopt or uninstall it in TikTok Shop, booking stops at once, we revoke our Moneybird access, and we delete the data of that shop (shop ID and shop cipher, tokens, settings, statuses, subscription status, order counts and any stored transactions) right away. Your subscription is cancelled at the end of the paid period.
  • Until our Moneybird access is revoked and your subscription is cancelled, a bare record remains with only the shop key, the date you disconnected, what is still to be done, and, only while those steps are open, our encrypted Moneybird token and your Polar customer ID. It is removed within 23 days, and database backups expire within 7 days after that, so everything is gone within 30 days. The one exception is a failed cancellation (section 5).
  • Journal entries already in Moneybird stay there: they are part of your own administration.
  • Invoices remain with Polar for as long as tax law requires.
  • You can ask us to delete your data sooner by emailing thijs@dortus.com.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete personal data, and to object to processing. Email thijs@dortus.com; we usually answer within one business day and handle the request within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top changes when we do, and we tell connected shops about material changes by email or in the app.

Kort in het Nederlands
