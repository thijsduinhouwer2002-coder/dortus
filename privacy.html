Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This policy explains how Dortus (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with our Atlassian Marketplace apps, including AskSpace.

1. Who we are

Dortus is an independent software vendor publishing apps on the Atlassian Marketplace. Contact: thijs.duinhouwer2002@gmail.com

2. What our apps do

AskSpace is a Confluence app built on Atlassian Forge. It lets people in a Confluence space ask questions, post answers, vote, mark a best answer, and receive in-app notifications.

3. Where data is stored

AskSpace runs entirely inside Atlassian’s cloud (Forge). Questions, answers, votes, settings, and in-app notifications are stored using Forge app storage in your Atlassian site. We do not operate a separate application server for AskSpace, and we do not send app content to Dortus’s own servers.

AskSpace is eligible for Atlassian’s Runs on Atlassian program: there is no required data egress to Dortus for normal use of the app.

4. What data the app processes

Depending on how you use AskSpace, the app may process:

  • Question and answer text you enter
  • Votes and “best answer” marks
  • Space settings (for example who may ask or answer)
  • Atlassian account identifiers needed to show authors and enforce permissions
  • Display names and avatar URLs provided by Atlassian APIs, so authors can be shown in the UI
  • In-app notification records (for example that someone answered your question)

We do not ask for payment card details inside the app. Marketplace billing is handled by Atlassian.

5. Why we process it

Only to provide the app features you enable: storing Q&A content, permissions, voting, and notifications.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. App data stays in your Atlassian environment under Atlassian’s platform and your organisation’s Atlassian admin controls. We may see limited information if you contact support (for example your email and what you write to us).

7. Retention

Content remains while the app is installed and the data is kept in Forge storage for your site. If you uninstall the app or delete content in the product, Atlassian’s uninstall/storage rules apply. Support emails we receive are kept only as long as needed to help you.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. For data inside Confluence/Forge, start with your Atlassian site admin. For questions to Dortus, email us at the address above.

9. Children

Our apps are intended for workplace use, not for children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
