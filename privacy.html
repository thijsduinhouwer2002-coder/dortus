2. Our apps

This policy covers Dortus apps on the Atlassian Marketplace, starting with AskSpace for Confluence Cloud. It also covers Arrears for Shopify, see section 9.

3. Data inside the app

AskSpace runs on Atlassian Forge and qualifies for Runs on Atlassian. Content you create in the app (questions, answers, votes, best-answer marks, tags, space settings and in-app notifications) is stored in Forge storage on your Atlassian site. Dortus does not operate servers for the app and has no access to this content.

To show authors and check permissions, the app uses Atlassian account IDs, display names and group memberships provided by Atlassian.

When a space admin moves questions from Questions for Confluence Cloud on the same site, AskSpace reads Confluence content and content properties for that import only. It does not change anything in Questions for Confluence.

4. Who is responsible

Your organisation decides how the app is used on its Atlassian site. Atlassian hosts and processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Data we do receive

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to help you, and delete it when it’s no longer needed for that, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Through the Atlassian Marketplace we receive licence information about your site (for example the site address and licence type), which we use for billing and support.

6. Logs

AskSpace only logs technical information, such as timings and error codes. It does not log question or answer text.

7. No selling, no tracking

We do not sell personal data. Our website uses no tracking cookies.

8. Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold (support emails). For content inside Confluence, contact your site admin. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

9. Arrears for Shopify

9.1 What the app does

Arrears is an embedded Shopify app for business-to-business collections. It reads your companies, company locations, contacts, orders, and payment terms through the Shopify Admin API, and turns them into aging views, statements, reminders, payment allocations, and credit holds.

9.2 Where it runs

Unlike our Confluence apps, Arrears needs a server. We host it on Fly.io in the Amsterdam region (EU), with the application database on a persistent volume attached to that machine. Access to the production environment is limited to Dortus.

9.3 Our role

For business data inside Arrears, you are the controller and Dortus is the processor. You decide which companies and contacts exist. We process that data only to deliver the features you switched on.

For your own contact with us (support email, billing questions), Dortus is the controller of that correspondence.

9.4 What data Arrears processes and stores

  • Your shop domain and the Shopify access token issued during install
  • Company names, company locations, and payment terms as configured in Shopify
  • Company contact names and email addresses, used to address statements and reminders
  • Order references, amounts, currencies, due dates, and payment schedule status
  • Reminder records: recipient address, subject, message body, send time, and result
  • Payment records you enter, including allocation across open invoices
  • Credit limits, hold and release events, and the staff action behind them
  • App settings, such as sender name, dunning policy, and automation switches

Arrears does not ask for or store payment card details. Subscription charges are handled by Shopify billing.

9.5 Protected customer data

Business contact data (company contact names and email addresses) is used only to produce and send the statements and reminders you request, and to show you who was contacted. We do not use it for our own marketing, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone outside the subprocessors listed in section 9.8.

9.6 Email sending

Statement and reminder emails are delivered through Resend. That means the recipient address, subject, and message body pass through Resend in order to be delivered, along with normal delivery metadata.

9.7 Shopify data requests and deletion

Arrears implements Shopify’s mandatory compliance webhooks:

  • customers/data_request: we respond to the merchant with the data we hold for the requested person.
  • customers/redact: we delete or anonymise the stored personal data for that person.
  • shop/redact: we delete the shop’s stored data after Shopify’s redaction window.

Uninstalling the app also revokes our access: the stored session for your shop is removed when we receive the app/uninstalled webhook.

9.8 Subprocessors

  • Shopify Inc.: platform, app hosting surface, and billing for Arrears.
  • Fly.io: application hosting and database storage for Arrears, EU (Amsterdam) region.
  • Resend: delivery of statement and reminder email from Arrears.

We do not use advertising networks, session recording, or third-party analytics inside our apps.

9.9 Why we process data

Only to provide the features you enable: showing aging, producing statements, sending the reminders you approve, allocating payments, and applying credit holds. Our legal basis is the performance of our agreement with you, and our legitimate interest in keeping the service running securely.

9.10 Retention

Arrears data is kept while the app is installed. It is deleted on shop redaction, and on request. Reminder history is kept so you can prove what was sent, until the shop data is deleted.

9.11 Security

Traffic to Arrears is served over HTTPS only. Platform credentials and API keys are held as environment secrets, not in source code. Access to production is limited to Dortus. We are a small vendor and we do not claim certifications we do not have: if you need a security review or a data processing agreement, email us and we will answer concretely.

9.12 Your rights

For data inside Arrears, start with the merchant that installed the app, or contact us directly.

9.13 International transfers

Arrears is hosted in the EU. Some subprocessors listed in section 9.8 are established outside the EU and may process limited data there under their own transfer mechanisms.

10. Changes

We update this page when things change and adjust the date above.
