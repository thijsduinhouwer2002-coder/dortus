MailView vs MSG/EML Email Viewer for Jira

Two Marketplace apps show Outlook .msg and .eml attachments on the work item. We installed both on the same Jira Cloud site and opened the same emails in each. This is what we saw, including what the other app does better.

Disclosure first: Dortus makes MailView. We did this test ourselves, on 16 October 2026, with MSG/EML Email Viewer for Jira version 2.2.0 by CloudScript and the MailView build that became 1.5 (1.6 differences are marked). If CloudScript has changed since, or we got something wrong, email support@dortus.com and we will correct it.

How we tested

One Jira Cloud site, one work item with nine test emails attached: a normal supplier email with an inline logo, an invoice scam with a misleading link, a reported phishing email with a lookalike domain, a forwarded email without a file extension, a broken file, and a few plain ones. Both apps installed side by side, each opened on the same work item.

What CloudScript does better

"Attach to work item" is useful: an agent gets an email with an invoice inside, and wants that invoice on the ticket as a normal attachment. In CloudScript that is one click. In MailView you download the file and upload it again. CloudScript also lives in the Activity tabs, which some teams will find more natural than a box in the right column.

What MailView does better

Reading: inline images show, earlier replies are folded, so a long thread reads like a mail client. Safety: a suspicious email gets a verdict with reasons, the Details tab shows what the receiving mail server recorded for SPF, DKIM and DMARC, and every link shows where it really goes. Trust: one read-only permission and nothing leaves your site; the email is parsed in your browser.

Which one fits

If your main job is pulling files out of emails onto tickets, CloudScript's attach button is a real time saver. If your desk reads forwarded customer emails, handles "is this mail legit?" tickets, or inherited a migration full of Outlook messages, MailView's reading and the phishing check matter more. Both have a free trial on the Marketplace, and installing both on a test site for an afternoon, as we did, answers the question for your own emails.

What about a browser extension?

There are free Chrome extensions that catch a downloaded .msg or .eml and open it in a tab. For one person on one laptop that works. For a service desk it has three catches: every agent has to install it themselves (and keep it after a laptop swap), it works only in that browser, and the email leaves Jira: the extension reads the full message, and you cannot see from the Marketplace or from your admin what it does with that. A Marketplace app is installed once by an admin, works for everyone, and shows its permissions on the listing.
