Getting started

  1. Install AskSpace from the Atlassian Marketplace. You need to be a Confluence admin.
  2. Open a space where you want Q&A, and choose AskSpace under Apps in the space sidebar.
  3. Turn it on. If you’re a space admin, click Turn on Q&A. Q&A is off by default in every space, so nothing changes for spaces you don’t turn on.
  4. Choose who may ask and answer (optional): go to Space settings › Integrations › AskSpace. By default, everyone who can view the space may ask and answer.
  5. Ask the first question, or add the AskSpace block to your space home page (see Add AskSpace to a page).
