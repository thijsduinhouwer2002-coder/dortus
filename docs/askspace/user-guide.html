User guide

Ask a question#

Click Ask a question. Give it a clear title, and add details and up to three tags if you like.

While you type the title, AskSpace shows similar questions that were already asked. Maybe your answer is already there.

Answer, vote and pick the best answer#

Post an answer under the question. Vote answers up or down to show what helped.

The person who asked can mark one answer as the best answer, but not their own answer. Moderators can do this too.

Find answers#

Use search at the top of the list. It looks through titles, details and answers.

Use the tabs to see all, unanswered, still open or solved questions. Click a tag to see every question with that tag.

For you#

The For you tab shows when someone answers your question, marks your answer as best, or when a moderator changes your post.

Share a question#

Open the … menu on a question and choose Copy link.

Formatting#

Questions and answers support:

  • links
  • **bold**
  • inline code between single backticks: `code`
  • code blocks between lines of three backticks: ```
  • lists starting with - or 1.

Edit or delete#

You can edit or delete your own questions and answers from the … menu.
