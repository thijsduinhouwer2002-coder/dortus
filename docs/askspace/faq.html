FAQ

Who can see the questions in a space?#

Everyone who can view that Confluence space and open the app.

Is Q&A turned on everywhere after installing?#

No. It’s off in every space until a space admin turns it on.

Can people earn points or badges?#

No. AskSpace has no points or leaderboards.

Can I export questions?#

Yes. Space admins can export all questions and answers of a space to CSV under Space settings › Integrations › AskSpace › Import & export.

Can I import from another Q&A app?#

Yes. Import a CSV file, a Questions for Confluence (Server/Data Center) export, or move from Questions for Confluence Cloud on the same site in one click. You see a preview first, and you can undo an import. See Import & export.

Do questions show up in Confluence search?#

Not yet. Use the search inside AskSpace, which also searches answers.

Which languages are supported?#

The app is in English.

What happens if I uninstall?#

The app and its data are handled according to Atlassian’s rules for Forge apps.
