Admin guide

Turn Q&A on or off#

Go to Space settings › Integrations › AskSpace. Turning Q&A off hides it in that space. Questions are kept and come back when you turn it on again.

Who may ask and who may answer#

For each, choose:

Everyone in the space
Anyone who can view this space.
Space admins only
People with admin permission for this space.
Chosen groups
Members of the Confluence groups you pick.

Asking and answering are set separately. A common setup is “everyone may ask, the support group answers”.

Moderators#

Space admins are always moderators. Add Confluence groups as moderators if other people should help.

Moderators can edit or delete any question or answer in the space, and choose or clear the best answer. When a moderator changes or deletes someone’s post, the author gets a notification, and an edited post shows “Edited by a moderator”.

Add AskSpace to a page#

In the Confluence editor, type /AskSpace and choose the AskSpace block. In its settings, choose:

  • what to show: Recent, Unanswered or Solved
  • how many questions: 3, 5 or 10
  • optionally, only questions with one tag

The block only shows questions from the space the page is in.

Import & export#

Space admins find import and export under Space settings › Integrations › AskSpace › Import & export.

Import questions#

  1. Choose a source: a CSV file in the Simple CSV format (see the columns below), a Questions for Confluence (Server/Data Center) export, or Questions for Confluence Cloud on the same site.
  2. Check the preview. You see what will be imported before you import it.
  3. Import.
  4. Undo if you need to. You can undo an import.

Moving from Questions for Confluence Cloud on the same site takes one click: choose it as the source.

Export to CSV#

Space admins can export all questions and answers of a space to CSV, whenever they want, under Space settings › Integrations › AskSpace › Import & export.

Limits#

  • 6,000 questions per space. When a space is full, nobody can ask a new question there, and AskSpace points to support.
  • 5,000 questions per import, with up to 100 answers per question.
  • If an import doesn’t fit in the space, AskSpace imports the newest questions that fit. The preview tells you how many don’t fit.
  • Importing again skips questions that were already imported into that space from the same source.

When you move from Questions for Confluence Cloud:

  • AskSpace only reads. Nothing changes in Questions for Confluence.
  • Answers added in Questions for Confluence after the import are not copied over. There is no sync.
  • Images, attachments and mentions of people become “[content omitted during import]”.
  • Headings become bold text, and italics are dropped.

Simple CSV columns#

ColumnRequiredHolds
titleYesThe question title
detailsNoMore text for the question
answerNoAn answer to the question
asked_byNoWho asked the question
answered_byNoWho answered it
asked_atNoWhen the question was asked
tagsNoTags for the question
