Data & privacy

Where is data stored?#

In Atlassian Forge storage for your site. AskSpace has no external servers and sends no data outside Atlassian (Runs on Atlassian).

What does AskSpace store?#

Questions, answers, votes, the best-answer mark, tags, space settings (on/off, who may ask, answer and moderate) and in-app notifications.

To show authors and check permissions, it uses Atlassian account IDs and display names. Avatars are shown as initials; no profile photos are loaded.

What permissions does the app ask for?#

  • Its own storage.
  • Reading spaces, to check who is a space admin.
  • Reading users, to show names.
  • Reading groups, for group-based permissions.

Can Dortus see our content?#

No. We have no access to your Forge storage. The app only logs technical information such as timings and error codes, never question or answer text.

See also the Privacy Policy.
