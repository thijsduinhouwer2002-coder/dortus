Click Joints Pro · Blender add-on for 3D printing

Rig to printable figure: elbows and knees stop where your rig stops.

Pick your rigged figure, press one button, and Click Joints Pro cuts it into printable parts with a joint at every shoulder, elbow, hip, knee and neck. Elbows and knees get a hinge with an end stop; shoulders, hips and neck get a ball joint. Next to each joint you see how far it bends, in degrees, before you print.

  • Figure from rig. One button reads your armature: hinges with an end stop at elbows and knees, ball joints at shoulders, hips and neck.
  • The end stop comes from your rig. A Limit Rotation constraint sets the angle (30° to 150°). No limit gives a preset per body part.
  • Every range in one view: a degree number at each joint, and a list with where each range came from.
  • Recognises common bone names: Rigify, Mixamo, Auto-Rig Pro, Unreal, Character Creator, VRoid, Biped and Daz.
  • No rig? Six body-part presets: place the 3D cursor and click Shoulder, Hip, Neck, Elbow, Knee or Ankle.
  • Hinge with pose slider, red where parts hit past the stop, and spacing checks between joints.
  • Everything from the free Click Joints: ball and pin joints, fit colour, bend range, STL and 3MF.

Measured in Blender, not print tested by us yet: print a test joint first. Works best on figures of about 200 mm or taller with separate arms and legs. Personal license $19 once, all Pro 1.x updates included.
