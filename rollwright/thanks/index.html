Thank you. Let’s turn your skins on.

Your purchase gives you a license key. It unlocks Parchment and Arcane on all your devices and it does not expire. Turning it on takes about a minute.

  1. Copy your key. Open your Polar purchases and sign in with the e-mail address you used to pay. Your license key is listed there with your purchase of Rollwright Skins. Copy it.
  2. Open the settings. In Obsidian go to Settings, then Rollwright under Community plugins.
  3. Activate. Paste the key in the License key field and press Activate. You need an internet connection for this one step.
  4. Pick a skin. Under Skins, choose Parchment or Arcane in the Skin list. The locks on their preview tiles are gone. Open a note with a roll: the whole note turns into the skin’s world. “Skin the note too” sets which notes change.

On another device you repeat steps 2 and 3 with the same key.

Something not working?

Does the plugin say the key is not valid, or can you not find your key? Write to thijs@dortus.com with the e-mail address you used to pay, and we sort it out. Questions about the payment itself, a receipt or a refund go through Polar, who handles the payment for us.
