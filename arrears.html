Arrears is no longer available

Arrears, our collections app for Shopify B2B, stopped on 14 November 2026. It is no longer listed on the Shopify App Store and can no longer be installed.

If you used Arrears and have a question about your data, write to support@dortus.com. What happens to Arrears data is described in section 14 of our privacy policy.
