Where most net-terms apps stop

Aging and reminder email are table stakes in this niche. The hard day for a collections desk is matching money and deciding who still gets credit.

Aging and reminders

Everyone ships a bucket view and a chase email. Useful, and not enough when a wire lands against five open invoices.

Cash application

Enter one bank amount. Arrears allocates oldest due first, keeps partials correct in the desk, and logs what changed. That is the job spreadsheets break on.

Credit hold in checkout

Over the limit, B2B checkout becomes a draft instead of new debt. Free includes manual holds; Pro can auto-hold when exposure crosses the line.

See debt. Chase it. Apply cash. Control new credit.

This is the whole product. If you need something outside this list, say so before you install and we will tell you straight.

  • Aging

    Total outstanding per company from Shopify payment schedules, split by days past due. Deep history past about 60 days of order age needs Shopify full-order access after App Store approval.

  • Statements

    A statement PDF per company with aging summary and open lines. Download it, or email it from the company desk.

  • Reminders

    A Ready queue of who to chase today, in sequence up to final notice. Every send is logged with the exact message. Starter adds daily auto-send.

  • Cash application

    One bank transfer, allocated across open invoices, oldest due first. Partials stay correct in Arrears even when Basic cannot store a partial on the Shopify order.

  • Credit holds

    Set a limit per company. Over the limit, B2B checkout becomes a draft order instead of new debt.

Pick the right tool

The niche is crowded with zero-review apps. Here is how we draw the line without pretending every merchant needs Arrears.

Read-only aging

If you only want a report and drafted reminder text, a lighter read-only app is enough. Arrears writes holds and allocates cash.

Credit-policy automation

If you need buyer self-serve finance pages and graded checkout policies as the main product, look at credit-specialist apps. Arrears holds via checkout-to-draft and keeps the desk simple.

Daily collections work

If staff need aging, statements, a Ready queue, cash application oldest due first, and an audit trail in one admin desk, that is Arrears.

What Arrears is not

Knowing the edges saves you an install.

Not a wholesale suite

No price lists, no order forms, no catalogue. Arrears starts once the order exists.

Not an invoicing platform

No legal invoice numbering, no PEPPOL, no e-invoicing compliance. Statements, not tax documents.

Not just extra emails

Reminders are one part. The value is the desk around them: aging, allocation, holds.
